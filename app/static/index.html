P95 WITHOUT THE MYSTERY

A spike isn't a sustained load.

For a complete UTC day, sort 288 five-minute peak buckets. Remove the largest 14 (70 minutes). The highest of the remaining 274 is the daily P95.

In the documented 2027 bursting model, add site daily P95s within each region. The highest daily sum in the month is the region's measured usage.
